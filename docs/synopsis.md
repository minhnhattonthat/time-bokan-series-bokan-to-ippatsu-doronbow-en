# Synopsis

Time Bokan Series: Bokan to Ippatsu! Doronbow (PS1, 1996, Banpresto / Eleven). A vertical shooter and slapstick
parody: the player is the villains. The Doronbow trio (Doronjo, Boyacky, Tonzra, from Yatterman) take on the heroes
of every Time Bokan show to prove they are the series' greatest villain trio (三悪). Everything spoken is in the 6
FMVs; the rest is labels, stage cards, credits and the sound test. One route, 7 stages (`docs/re/state.md`).

1. MOVE01, opening (before the title): Boyacky has already sent challenge letters to every hero: Yatterman,
   Zenderman, Rescueman, Yattodetaman, Ippatsuman, Itadakiman. Doronjo has doubts; Tonzra and Boyacky talk her into
   it, and she vows to steal the starring role. Title call.
2. MOVE02, new game, before ship select: the mecha are the gang's old ones (Time Gaikottsu, Skull Mecha, Andromedama,
   Time Rakuda, Skull Buggy, Denden Mecha), rebuilt inside as high-tech machines. Pick one.
3. Stages 1-3. Cards: 1 The Hawk Has Landed (Omotchama, Time Bokan's "Koron"), 2 Yatter King, 3 High Noon vs
   Zenderman.
4. MOVE03, after stage 3: every other villain trio had a machine of its own; the Doronbow only had a bicycle.
   Boyacky unveils the Genius Doronbow Special Machine, modeled on their faces. Ship select again.
5. Stages 4-7: helper mecha on the snowfield, Daikyojin (Yattodetaman), the double heroes, the air battle
   (Perasuke's "Peccha").
6. MOVE04, after stage 7: the heroes' giant mecha seem beaten. Nobody wants to press the final missile button;
   Boyacky's "click" lands on Doronjo first. They win, toast their victory... and something big appears.
7. MOVE05, at sunset: they lost after all. Tonzra blames his toast. Doronjo is happy she got to run wild with them
   once more; next time, total victory. "We're geniuses!"
8. Ending, staff roll, MOVE06: Dokurobey hands out a "loving punishment", music starts, the skull blast. THE END.

Late turn to protect: the victory in MOVE04 is undone at its very end; MOVE05 opens on the loss.

Running jokes: Boyacky's button press ("Annnd... click!"); the gang's reply "Arahora-sassa!"; the trio's wish to be
the stars; Doronjo's "Let 'em have it!"; Dokurobey's punishment.
